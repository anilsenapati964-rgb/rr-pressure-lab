Pressure definition, ratings and caveats

When is a delivery under pressure?

Chase: in the final 10 overs, with a target remaining, if required rate is at least 9, five or fewer wickets remain, or 18 or fewer balls remain. First innings: last five overs with four or more wickets down, or the final two overs regardless of wickets. Revised targets/overs are used where supplied.

How are the ranks scored?

Batting net rate = runs minus 20 run-equivalents per dismissal, per 100 balls. Bowling quality = 100 minus runs conceded per 100 legal balls, plus 20 per wicket per 100 legal balls. Each score adds a sample-shrunk pressure-versus-baseline difference; 50 balls is the shrinkage constant.

Eligibility

Players must have appeared in an IPL playing XI in the supplied period. Batter minimum: 100 pressure and 100 baseline balls. Bowler minimum: 120 pressure and 120 baseline legal balls. Each listed player also has at least 10 pressure matches.

Interpret with care

Thresholds and wicket value are model choices. IPL and T20I records are pooled for player rates; opposition, venue, role and match quality are not adjusted. Check auction registration, contracts, fitness and roster needs before treating the shortlist as a bid plan.
